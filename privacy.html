Policy draft — review required

This page is a launch draft, not a substitute for a finalized policy reviewed for EZ Screening's operating practices and applicable law.

Website use

The storefront currently stores cart selections in your browser's local storage. It does not create an account, submit contact forms, process payment cards, or transmit incident intake forms.

Information you choose to enter

Forms are presented as drafts until secure submission services are configured. Avoid entering sensitive personal or medical data into unconnected forms.

Payment and account services

Before enabling checkout or account creation, this policy must be updated to identify the applicable processors, data retention practices, security controls, and contact information.

Your choices

You can clear your cart from the cart page or by clearing site data in your browser. This draft does not describe any server-side customer record.

Future updates

EZ Screening should publish a reviewed, effective-dated privacy notice before collecting customer, payment, or incident information.
