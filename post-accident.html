Understanding
Post-Accident Testing.

Post-accident testing may be required under an employer policy or federal regulations. Prioritize emergency medical care, site safety, and the appropriate testing timeline.

Requirements, eligible collections, and result turnaround vary by program. Confirm the testing method and any required documentation before purchasing.
