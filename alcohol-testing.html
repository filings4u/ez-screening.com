ABOUT THIS SERVICE

Understanding
Alcohol Testing.

Alcohol screening services may use breath testing or laboratory analysis of a biological specimen. The methods answer different questions and are not interchangeable.

Requirements, eligible collections, and result turnaround vary by program. Confirm the testing method and any required documentation before purchasing.

01

Breath screening

Measures breath alcohol concentration using appropriate equipment and procedures.

02

EtG laboratory testing

Tests for an alcohol metabolite; does not provide a current breath alcohol concentration.

03

Timing matters

Post-accident and regulated testing may have timing and documentation rules.
