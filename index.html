05 / FAQ

Questions?
We can help.

Have a specific test requirement? Start with our services or contact the current Screenings4U team.

What testing methods can I explore?

Common types include urine, hair, oral fluid, and alcohol testing. The appropriate method depends on the testing requirement.

Can employers request testing?

Employer testing may cover pre-employment, random, post-accident, and other workplace needs.

Is DOT testing different from non-DOT?

Yes. DOT programs follow specific federal procedures. A non-DOT test cannot replace a required DOT test.

How long do results take?

Turnaround varies by service, lab processing, and required review. Confirm timing for the specific test.
